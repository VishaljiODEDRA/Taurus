# Taurus Outreach Pack

## Purpose

This outreach pack supports the next phase of Taurus: building serious technical relationships early, before the project needs formal references, pilots, investors, or commercial partners.

The goal is to ask credible people for feedback over time, not endorsements, recommendation letters, visa help, investment, or public praise.

Use this document to:

- introduce Taurus clearly,
- ask for specific technical feedback,
- show the project as fintech infrastructure,
- avoid investment advice or performance claims,
- track relationships privately,
- prepare for future fundraising without leading with fundraising.

## Project Summary

Taurus is a risk-first AI trading governance platform.

The current repository is a local Python governance engine for AI-assisted trading workflows. It includes:

- shadow/demo/live execution modes with strict live safety gates,
- eToro integration,
- deterministic risk engine,
- SQLite ledger,
- JSONL audit logs,
- backtesting,
- walk-forward validation,
- model training and promotion/rejection controls,
- reliability reports,
- news scoring,
- broker reconciliation,
- emergency kill switch.

The product thesis is:

AI can research, score, rank, and explain. Deterministic governance must decide whether anything is allowed.

Taurus is not a profit bot, investment adviser, copy-trading service, managed account, or performance-promise product. It is infrastructure for paper/demo trading validation, model governance, broker reconciliation, auditability, and future compliance-aware fintech workflows.

### First Outreach Sequence

1. Start with 20 reachable names from events, open-source discussions, newsletters, podcasts, and programme pages.
2. Send 10 careful messages over two weeks, using one narrow question per person.
3. Prioritize replies from people who challenge the architecture, safety case, or market category.
4. Ask good responders for one suggested reviewer only after they have seen the repo or demo.
5. By September 2026, aim for 3 serious relationships with people who have followed the work over multiple updates.

## Outreach Cadence

Suggested June-September cadence:

- Week 1: identify 20 possible reviewers, select 10 priority targets.
- Week 2: send 3 carefully personalised messages.
- Week 3: send 3 more messages and follow up with first group.

## Relationship Quality Bar

A serious relationship is not a single reply.

Count it as serious only if:

- they gave specific feedback,
- you acted on at least one point,
- they responded again or accepted a follow-up,
- they understand the project as governance infrastructure,
- they could plausibly comment on your technical execution by September 2026.

## Final Safety Reminder

Every outreach message should preserve the Taurus product boundary:

- technical feedback, not endorsement,
- governance infrastructure, not trading performance,
- paper/demo validation, not public live execution,
- risk and auditability, not profit screenshots,
- private CRM, not public relationship mining.
